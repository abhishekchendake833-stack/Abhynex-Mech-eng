Engineering Mechanics Level 1

Q1. Newton's second law is represented by:

Answer: B. F = ma

Newton's second law states that the resultant force acting on a body is equal to mass multiplied by acceleration.

F = Force (N)
m = Mass (kg)
a = Acceleration (m/s²)

Therefore: 1 N = 1 kg·m/s²

Engineering Mechanics Numerical

Q2. A 10 kg body accelerates at 3 m/s². Find the force.

Answer: C. 30 N

Given:

m = 10 kg
a = 3 m/s²

Formula:

F = ma

Calculation:

F = 10 × 3

F = 30 N

Engineering Check: kg × m/s² = N, so the unit is correct.

Strength of Materials Conceptual

Q3. Normal stress is defined as:

Answer: A. Force / Area

Normal stress:

σ = F / A

Stress represents the intensity of internal resisting force per unit cross-sectional area.

SI unit = Pa = N/m²

Engineering applications: shafts, beams, columns, bolts, plates and pressure vessels.

Thermodynamics Conceptual

Q4. First law of thermodynamics is based on:

Answer: A. Conservation of energy

For a closed system, using the convention that W is work done by the system:

Q - W = ΔU

Heat supplied to the system can appear as increase in internal energy and/or work output.

Applications: engines, compressors, turbines, boilers, refrigerators and power plants.

Fluid Mechanics Numerical

Q5. Water flows through an area of 0.02 m² at 4 m/s. Find discharge.

Answer: B. 0.08 m³/s

Formula:

Q = A × V

A = 0.02 m²
V = 4 m/s

Q = 0.02 × 4

Q = 0.08 m³/s

This equation represents volumetric flow rate for the stated steady one-dimensional average-flow situation.

Manufacturing Basic

Q6. CNC stands for:

Answer: B. Computer Numerical Control

CNC machines use programmed numerical instructions to control machine-tool movements and operations.

Typical learning areas: coordinate systems, G-codes, M-codes, tool offsets, work offsets, feeds, speeds, tool paths and simulation.

Theory of Machines Conceptual

Q7. The main purpose of a flywheel is to:

Answer: A. Reduce cyclic speed fluctuation

A flywheel stores and releases rotational kinetic energy during a cycle.

E = ½ Iω²

It helps smooth cyclic variations in speed caused by variations in turning moment.

Heat Transfer Conceptual

Q8. Heat transfer through a solid due to molecular interaction is called:

Answer: A. Conduction

For one-dimensional steady conduction through a plane wall with constant thermal conductivity:

Q = kA(T₁ − T₂) / L

Heat flows from higher temperature toward lower temperature.

Machine Design Conceptual

Q9. Factor of Safety is generally related to:

Answer: A

Factor of Safety provides a margin between a relevant failure strength and the chosen allowable/working stress. The exact definition depends on the failure criterion and design standard.

Example for a simple tensile-yield design:

FoS = Yield Stress / Allowable Stress

📐 Important Automobile & EV Formulas

Mechanical Power P = T × ω For rotational systems.
Power from RPM P = 2πNT / 60 N = rpm, T = torque.
Vehicle Kinetic Energy KE = ½mv² Energy due to vehicle motion.
Wheel Tractive Force F ≈ Tw / rw Approximate relation ignoring losses.
Battery Energy E(Wh) = V × Ah Approximate nominal electrical energy.
EV Range Range ≈ Usable Energy / Consumption Units must be consistent.
Stopping Distance d = v² / 2a Ideal constant-deceleration model.
Heat Energy Q = mcΔT Used in thermal calculations.
Efficiency η = Output / Input Usually expressed as a percentage.
Gear Ratio i = Input Speed / Output Speed Convention must be stated clearly.
